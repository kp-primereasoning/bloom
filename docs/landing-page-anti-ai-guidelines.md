# Landing Page Anti-AI Design Guidelines

Rules to ensure the Bloom landing page doesn't look AI-generated. Every item is a concrete, checkable requirement.

---

## Layout & Structure

- Break the "hero → 3-col features → testimonials → pricing → footer" skeleton. Reorder sections, merge them, or drop one entirely.
- Introduce at least one asymmetric layout block. Offset an image, use an uneven column split (e.g. 7/5 instead of 6/6), or let one element bleed off-grid.
- Vary vertical spacing between sections. Use noticeably different gaps (e.g. 80px after hero, 120px before CTA, 48px between feature rows) instead of uniform `padding-y`.
- Avoid exactly 3 feature cards in a row. Use 2, 4, or a staggered/bento layout instead.
- Add at least one non-standard section type: a scrolling marquee, an inline video, an interactive element, a comparison table, or a hand-drawn annotation.
- Don't center-align everything. Mix left-aligned text blocks with centered headings, or right-align a pull quote.

## Typography

- Use no more than 2 typefaces, but make sure at least one is not Inter, DM Sans, or Poppins.
- Vary font weights within headings. Mix a light weight with a bold word for emphasis instead of uniform `font-weight: 700`.
- Set body text to 17–19px, not exactly 16px.
- Use real typographic details: proper em dashes (—), curly quotes (" "), and non-breaking spaces before units.

## Images & Visuals

- Use real photography of the actual product, team, or environment. Zero tolerance for AI-generated people.
- If using illustrations, maintain a single consistent style across every illustration on the page.
- Avoid the "diverse team high-fiving in a bright office" stock photo genre entirely.
- Check all images for: warped background text, inconsistent shadow directions, and objects that fade into nothing at edges (AI image artifacts).
- Use images with real environmental context: actual locations, real product in someone's hands, behind-the-scenes shots.

## Copy & Tone

- **Banned words/phrases:** "delve," "elevate," "seamlessly," "leverage," "unlock," "cutting-edge," "in today's fast-paced world," "at its core," "comprehensive solution," "robust," "empower," "foster," "beacon," "tapestry," "game-changer," "dive into," "unlock the potential."
- Write at least one sentence per section that could only be true about Bloom specifically.
- Include concrete numbers, dates, or proper nouns. "Since March 2024" or "47 properties" signals a real human wrote it.
- Vary sentence length deliberately. Throw in a 4-word sentence. Then a 40-word one.
- Don't open every section with a dramatic rhetorical question or "Imagine a world where..." framing.
- Avoid perfectly parallel structure in feature lists (e.g. "Verb + your + noun" repeated three times).
- Include a real human voice somewhere: a founder quote with casual tone, a customer testimonial with imperfect grammar, or a line that's slightly funny.
